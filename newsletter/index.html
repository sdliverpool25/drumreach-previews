Who it's for1

  • Core reader: anyone in an Irish workplace who uses AI day to day, or wants to, in any firm of any size, anywhere in Ireland.
  • Typical roles: finance, operations, admin, marketing, sales, HR and managers. Also the person who has become "the AI person" in the office.
  • Also welcome: owners and MDs who want their team to get further with AI.

The promise2

Take you from level 1 to level 4 of AI adoption, one practical step a week. Every issue gives you something you can try at work that week.

The 4 levels3

  1. Level 1, Trying it: you use ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini now and then, for one-off questions.
  2. Level 2, Daily use: you use AI every day with good prompts, your own context and a human check.
  3. Level 3, Workflows: you turn repeat tasks into set steps, using saved prompts, templates, custom GPTs or Copilot agents.
  4. Level 4, Automations and agents: AI runs tasks for you, triggered by email, forms or a schedule (for example Power Automate, Zapier, Make or an agent), and a person reviews the results.

Each issue is tagged with the level it suits, so readers can tell what's for them.

Content pillars4

  • Workflows: step-by-step ways to do a real work task with AI.
  • Automations: set it up once and let it run.
  • Tools: what's worth using, and what it costs in euro.
  • Tricks: prompts and settings that make results better.
  • Hacks: small shortcuts that save minutes every day.

Issue format (about 5 minutes)5

  1. The level tag and a one-line promise ("By the end you'll have…").
  2. This week's move: one workflow, automation, tool, trick or hack, with the steps.
  3. Copy this: a prompt or template.
  4. Irish note (light): one line on Irish context where it fits, such as a LEO or Enterprise Ireland support, the AI Act, GDPR or a euro price.
  5. One question: reply with what you tried.

Rules: confirm prices and facts on the source page in the week of sending. Link to the source. Label any example that isn't real as illustrative.

Irish context and case studies (light)6

  • Irish context: a one-line Irish note per issue, only when it's relevant.
  • Case studies: an occasional "How [Firm] uses AI" feature, roughly monthly, from an Irish workplace. Outreach goes from a DrumReach domain. The interviewee approves their quotes and figures, and Shane approves everything before it's sent or published.
  • The 5 questions: where do you use AI, what did it fix, what changed, what surprised you, what should a peer do first?

Growth8

  • Opt-in only. Use Beehiiv double opt-in and never import lists.
  • LinkedIn: 3 posts a week (the issue, one trick, one tool or hack). The featured link goes to the quiz.
  • Personal invites: about 100 one-to-one invites to people in Irish workplaces who use AI.
  • Partners: accountants, MSPs, chambers and LEOs share the quiz.
  • Case-study firms share their own feature.
  • Beehiiv referrals and recommendations from Issue 2. No paid ads yet.

Lead magnet: "What level are you?" quiz9

  • 8–10 quick questions (for example "How often do you use AI at work?" and "Do you have saved prompts?").
  • The result is Level 1, 2, 3 or 4, with three next steps and the best past issues for that level.
  • Beehiiv tags each subscriber by level, so it's possible to send to one level later.
  • A Digital Drumm readiness call is offered to Level 3–4 readers and to team leads.
