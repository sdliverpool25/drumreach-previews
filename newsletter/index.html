Who it's for1

  • People in Irish workplaces who use AI at work.
  • Any job, any size of firm, anywhere in Ireland.

What it does for them2

  • Gets you from level 1 to level 4 with AI.
  • One thing to try at work each week.
  • The job it does (Inbox Collective's terms): education and action.

The 4 levels3

  1. Try: you ask a chat AI the odd question.
  2. Daily: you use it every day, with good prompts.
  3. Workflows: you save steps for tasks you repeat.
  4. Automations: AI does tasks for you, and you check the results.

What we write about4

  • Workflows
  • Automations
  • Tools
  • Tricks
  • Hacks

Each issue5

  1. The level it's for.
  2. One thing to try, step by step.
  3. A prompt to copy.
  4. An Irish note, if it fits.
  5. A question: "What did you try?"

The Irish touch (light)6

  • Irish supports, rules and euro prices, only when they're useful.
  • About once a month: "How [Firm] uses AI". The firm approves its quotes, and Shane approves before anything is sent.
