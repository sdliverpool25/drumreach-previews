Who it's for1

  • People in Irish workplaces who use AI at work.
  • Any job, any size of firm, anywhere in Ireland.

What it does for them2

  • Shows how Shane actually uses AI at work: real use cases, built in public.
  • Each use case is tagged with a level from 1 to 4, so you can copy what suits you.
  • The job it does (Inbox Collective's terms): education and action.

The 4 levels3

  1. Try: you ask a chat AI the odd question.
  2. Daily: you use it every day, with good prompts.
  3. Workflows: you save steps for tasks you repeat.
  4. Automations: AI does tasks for you, and you check the results.

Each use case4

  • What: the job Shane used AI for.
  • Tools: what he used (for example Grok Bots, Microsoft Copilot or Base44).
  • Steps: how he did it, short and plain.
  • Time saved: only if Shane gives a real number.
  • Level: 1–4.

Each issue5

  1. 2–4 of Shane's real use cases.
  2. Irish AI scene: events, podcasts and videos worth your time.
  3. A question: "What did you try?"

Rule: no made-up results, numbers or quotes.

Irish AI scene (in every issue)6

  • 2–4 events, podcasts or videos from Ireland, each with a link that has been checked.
  • Sometimes a "How [Firm] uses AI" story. The firm approves its quotes, and Shane approves before anything is sent.

Sources to check each week - AI Ireland podcast (Mark Kelly): https://aiireland.ie/podcast/ - AI Ireland on YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@aiawardsirl - Newstalk Breakfast Business podcast: https://www.newstalk.com/podcasts/breakfast-business/are-irish-businesses-leading-the-ai-revolution - Newstalk Down to Business: https://podcasts.apple.com/ie/podcast/down-to-business/id250490423 - Silicon Republic, Machines: https://www.siliconrepublic.com/machines/ - Meetup groups: PyData Ireland, ODSC AI Dublin, Dublin Atlassian Community - Eventbrite Ireland (search "AI") - Nomad AI events: https://events.nomad.sc/e/waypoint-cork-inaugural
