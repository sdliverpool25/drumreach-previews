The diagnostic

5 places Tricel could be bleeding time and money

Answer honestly and tick the box if it applies. The figures are illustrative estimates based on stated assumptions — not your actual numbers, which we don't have.

AREA 01

CRM data nobody fully trusts

If two people pull 'pipeline by market', do they get the same number?

LOOK FOR
  • Duplicate accounts and contacts
  • Stages used differently by market
  • Missing close reasons and values
  • Manual clean-ups before every review
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 5 staff each spend 2 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 40/hr: 5 × 2 × EUR 40 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 1,730/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 02

Metrics defined differently everywhere

Is 'win rate' or 'lead' the same thing in every country and business unit?

LOOK FOR
  • Definitions in individual spreadsheets
  • Reports that can't be compared across markets
  • Board packs reconciled by hand
  • No metric dictionary
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 3 staff each spend 3 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 45/hr: 3 × 3 × EUR 45 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 1,750/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 03

Quotes that go quiet

Which open quotes are most likely to close — and which deserve a follow-up call this week?

LOOK FOR
  • Follow-up order set by date, not likelihood
  • No pattern analysis on lost quotes
  • Pricing decisions without win/loss data
  • Sales time spent on low-probability deals
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If prioritising follow-ups won just 1 extra order a month with an assumed EUR 3,000 margin, that's ≈ EUR 3,000/month (illustrative assumption).
AREA 04

Market intelligence gathered by hand

How do you track distributors, competitors and tenders across 50+ countries?

LOOK FOR
  • Desk research repeated per market
  • Competitor price checks ad hoc
  • Distributor lists out of date
  • Tender portals checked manually
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 2 staff each spend 4 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 40/hr: 2 × 4 × EUR 40 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 1,390/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 05

New data team starting from zero

How long until a new data manager delivers their first trusted dashboard?

LOOK FOR
  • First months spent finding and cleaning data
  • Knowledge of systems scattered
  • No baseline to measure progress
  • Stakeholders waiting for 'the single source'
Estimated bleed (illustrative): Qualitative — the cost is months of a senior hire's time spent on groundwork. No estimate.
