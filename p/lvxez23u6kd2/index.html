The diagnostic

5 places Gleneagle could be bleeding time and money

Answer honestly and tick the box if it applies. The figures are illustrative estimates based on stated assumptions — not your actual numbers, which we don't have.

AREA 01

Event nights priced like ordinary nights

How early do the hotels know about a big arena night — and does pricing move in time?

LOOK FOR
  • Event calendar shared by email
  • Rates adjusted late or by feel
  • Minimum stays not used on peak nights
  • Neighbouring properties pricing ahead of you
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If an extra EUR 15 average rate on 200 room-nights a month around events, that's ≈ EUR 3,000/month (illustrative assumption).
AREA 02

Rosters that don't track demand

Are staffing levels set from a forecast, or from last year's rota?

LOOK FOR
  • Rotas copied from the previous week
  • Overstaffing on quiet nights, scrambling on busy ones
  • Banqueting and bar staffing set late
  • Labour % reviewed after the month closes
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If better forecasting trimmed 40 surplus hours a week at an assumed EUR 16/hr, that's 40 × 16 × 4.33 ≈ EUR 2,770/month (illustrative assumption).
AREA 03

Reviews read, not analysed

What are the top three fixable complaints at each property this quarter?

LOOK FOR
  • Reviews answered one by one
  • No themes tracked over time
  • Ops teams don't see trends
  • Same issue mentioned for months
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 3 staff each spend 2 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 25/hr: 3 × 2 × EUR 25 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 650/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 04

Reports built by hand every week

How long does the weekly and monthly group reporting take to assemble?

LOOK FOR
  • Figures pulled from several property systems
  • Spreadsheets merged manually
  • Commentary written from scratch
  • Late numbers delaying decisions
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 2 staff each spend 4 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 35/hr: 2 × 4 × EUR 35 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 1,210/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 05

Guest data that doesn't drive marketing

Do you know which guests are likely to return for an event — and invite them?

LOOK FOR
  • One-size-fits-all newsletters
  • No link between arena tickets and hotel stays
  • Repeat guests not recognised
  • Campaign results not measured
Estimated bleed (illustrative): Hard to quantify without your data — qualitative only.
