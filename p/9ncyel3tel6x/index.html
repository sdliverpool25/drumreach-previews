The diagnostic

5 places EPS could be bleeding time and money

Answer honestly and tick the box if it applies. The figures are illustrative estimates based on stated assumptions — not your actual numbers, which we don't have.

AREA 01

Tender documents read line by line

"How many engineer-hours go into reading tender documents and specs before anyone starts pricing?"

LOOK FOR
  • Specs and clarifications read end-to-end for every bid
  • Compliance matrices built by hand in Excel
  • Risk clauses spotted late — or after award
  • The same questions re-answered tender after tender
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 3 staff each spend 6 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 55/hr: 3 × 6 × EUR 55 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 4,290/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 02

Past bids you can't search

When a similar scheme comes up, can anyone find what EPS said, priced and learned last time — in minutes?

LOOK FOR
  • Old submissions in folders by project number
  • Knowledge held by a few senior estimators
  • Lessons-learned not linked to new bids
  • Copy-paste from the last bid that 'looked similar'
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 4 staff each spend 2 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 50/hr: 4 × 2 × EUR 50 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 1,730/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 03

O&M data that only raises alarms after the fact

Do plant readings tell you a pump is degrading, or only that it has failed?

LOOK FOR
  • Alarms reviewed reactively
  • Maintenance by calendar rather than condition
  • Telemetry stored but rarely analysed
  • Call-outs that a trend would have predicted
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If better trend-spotting avoided just 2 emergency call-outs a month at an assumed EUR 900 each (labour, travel, parts), that's ≈ EUR 1,800/month (illustrative assumption; telemetry access may sit with the client).
AREA 04

Reports assembled from three systems

How long does the monthly project or contract report take when the numbers live in IFS, Sage and spreadsheets?

LOOK FOR
  • Exports from ERP re-shaped in Excel
  • Commentary written from scratch each month
  • Project managers chasing the same figures
  • Late or inconsistent client reporting
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 10 staff each spend 1.5 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 45/hr: 10 × 1.5 × EUR 45 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 2,920/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 05

Growth outpacing process

With a 2030 plan and a growing pipeline, which manual steps break first?

LOOK FOR
  • Onboarding new engineers takes months to reach speed
  • Procedures live in people's heads
  • Hiring to absorb admin, not engineering
  • No AI roadmap tied to the strategic plan
Estimated bleed (illustrative): Qualitative — the cost shows up as hiring for admin capacity rather than engineering capacity. No estimate.
