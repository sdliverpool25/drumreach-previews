The diagnostic

5 places MIG could be bleeding time and money

Answer honestly and tick the box if it applies. The figures are illustrative estimates based on stated assumptions — not your actual numbers, which we don't have.

AREA 01

Renewals that walk quietly

Which of next month's renewals are most likely to lapse — and does each branch know before the customer rings a competitor?

LOOK FOR
  • Renewal lists worked in date order, not risk order
  • Lapse reasons captured as free text or not at all
  • No early flag when a long-standing customer goes quiet
  • Retention reports that look backwards, not forwards
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 16 branches each lose just 2 renewals a month that a timely call would have saved, at an assumed EUR 60 average commission, that's 32 × EUR 60 ≈ EUR 1,920/month in commission (illustrative assumption, not your figures).
AREA 02

Cover gaps nobody spots

"How many motor-only customers also own a business, a farm or a home you don't insure?"

LOOK FOR
  • Cross-sell left to individual broker memory
  • Customer records split by product line
  • No 'next best product' prompt at renewal
  • Campaigns sent to everyone rather than likely buyers
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If a targeted list produced just 10 extra policies a month at an assumed EUR 120 commission each, that's ≈ EUR 1,200/month (illustrative assumption).
AREA 03

Quote and claims documents retyped by hand

How many times is the same detail read from a PDF or email and keyed into a system?

LOOK FOR
  • Proposal forms and schedules re-entered manually
  • Claims emails triaged by whoever is free
  • Insurer documents compared line by line
  • Attachments saved, renamed and filed by hand
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 6 staff each spend 3 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 30/hr: 6 × 3 × EUR 30 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 2,340/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 04

Dashboards that report but don't recommend

Your Power BI shows what happened. Does it tell a branch manager what to do on Monday morning?

LOOK FOR
  • Reports opened at month-end, not daily
  • Branch comparisons without explanation
  • No alerts pushed into Teams when something changes
  • Decisions still made on gut feel
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 16 staff each spend 1 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 35/hr: 16 × 1 × EUR 35 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 2,420/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 05

AI use without guard-rails

"Is anyone already pasting customer details into a public AI chatbot?"

LOOK FOR
  • No written AI usage policy
  • No view of which AI tools staff use
  • Customer data rules not mapped to AI use
  • No DPIA template for new models
Estimated bleed (illustrative): Hard to put a number on — but in a Central Bank-regulated, GDPR-heavy business, a single data incident costs far more than prevention (qualitative; no estimate).
