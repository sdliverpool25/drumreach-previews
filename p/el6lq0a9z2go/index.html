The diagnostic

5 places Barry Group could be bleeding time and money

Answer honestly and tick the box if it applies. The figures are illustrative estimates based on stated assumptions — not your actual numbers, which we don't have.

AREA 01

Orders keyed in by hand

How many retailer orders still arrive by phone, email or PDF and get typed into the system?

LOOK FOR
  • Orders transcribed from emails or photos
  • Phone orders taken on paper first
  • Errors fixed after picking, not before
  • Order desk overloaded on peak days
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 4 staff each spend 8 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 28/hr: 4 × 8 × EUR 28 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 3,880/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 02

Forecasting by gut and last year

Is replenishment driven by a forecast, or by what was ordered this week last year?

LOOK FOR
  • Buyers adjusting spreadsheets by eye
  • Promotions and weather not modelled
  • Same safety stock for fast and slow lines
  • Forecast accuracy not measured
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If better forecasting cut write-offs and expedited orders by just EUR 2,500 a month across the range, that's EUR 2,500/month (illustrative assumption — your actual waste figures would set this).
AREA 03

Stock-outs at one end, slow movers at the other

When a retailer can't get a line, do you know — and do you know what it cost?

LOOK FOR
  • Short-picks not tracked as lost sales
  • Dead stock reviewed quarterly at best
  • Allocation in shortages done manually
  • No alert when a fast line runs low
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 2 staff each spend 4 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 32/hr: 2 × 4 × EUR 32 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 1,110/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 04

Franchise data sitting idle

Your banners generate rich sales data. Does each store get insight back — or just an invoice?

LOOK FOR
  • Store performance compared only on totals
  • No range advice per store
  • Retailers asking for reports you build by hand
  • Insights not used in recruitment of new franchisees
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If 2 staff each spend 5 hrs/week on this at an assumed EUR 35/hr: 2 × 5 × EUR 35 × 4.33 weeks ≈ EUR 1,520/month. Illustrative assumption, not your figures.
AREA 05

Delivery routes planned from memory

Are routes and drop sequences optimised, or inherited?

LOOK FOR
  • Routes rarely re-planned as customers change
  • Part-full trucks on some runs
  • Delivery windows agreed ad hoc
  • Fuel cost not tracked per drop
Estimated bleed (illustrative): If optimisation saved 3% on an assumed EUR 60,000/month transport cost, that's ≈ EUR 1,800/month (illustrative assumption).
